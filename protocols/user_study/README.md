# Future user study protocol

Status: `planned_not_run`. This package is a preregistration-ready design and contains no participant records or results.

The study will compare a strong local-explanation baseline (prediction, calibrated confidence, top SHAP-style reasons and a standard limitation) with the FuzzyXAI human card (decision, reasons, contradictions, reliability, action and expandable provenance). It is not evidence for current user-comprehension, overtrust or domain-safety claims.

## Primary outcomes

1. Correct identification of the explanation limitation.
2. Correct operational action (`accept`, `review`, or `block`) under the supplied contract.
3. Overtrust: accepting an intentionally insufficient explanation as conclusive.

Secondary outcomes are reason comprehension, response time, confidence and perceived workload.

## Required execution boundary

The interface, scenarios, randomization, exclusions, primary outcomes and statistical plan must be frozen before recruitment. Raw individual responses must be retained in anonymized form. No result may be imported into Chapter 4 without an ethics/consent decision and a signed study lock.
