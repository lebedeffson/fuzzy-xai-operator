# Participant information and consent template

This is a template for a future non-clinical research study. It must be reviewed under the applicable institutional process before recruitment.

Participants will compare two forms of machine-learning explanation and answer comprehension and action-selection questions. The system does not provide medical, financial or legal advice. Participation is voluntary; a participant may stop before submission without penalty. Responses are stored under an anonymous identifier and analyzed in aggregate. The final study materials must specify retention time, contact details, compensation and withdrawal procedure before use.

Consent fields:

- I am at least 18 years old.
- I have read the study information.
- I understand that the explanations are research materials, not professional advice.
- I consent to anonymous analysis of my responses.
