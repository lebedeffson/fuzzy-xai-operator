# H10-C4 Supplementary Evidence

H10-C4 is a prospective experiment on controlled structural mutations. It is
separate from H10-C3 and does not evaluate natural incidents or human factors.

The complete machine-readable tables are:

- `results/h10_c4/SCENARIO_MANIFEST.csv`;
- `results/h10_c4/DEVELOPMENT_COST_CALIBRATION.json`;
- `results/h10_c4/PLAN_LEVEL_RESULTS.csv`;
- `results/h10_c4/STRATEGY_COMPARISON.csv`;
- `results/h10_c4/PIPELINE_FAMILY_RESULTS.csv`;
- `results/h10_c4/COST_MODEL_COMPARISON.csv`;
- `results/h10_c4/COST_SENSITIVITY.csv`;
- `results/h10_c4/BOOTSTRAP_INTERVALS.csv`;
- `results/h10_c4/HOLM_CORRECTION.csv`;
- `results/h10_c4/SELECTION_STABILITY.csv`.

The current source DOCX contains tables 4.1-4.7. Therefore, the candidate
numbers 4.11, 4.13, 4.15, 4.17, 4.20, 4.21, 4.28-4.30 and 4.36-4.38 listed in
the revision brief do not exist in this edition and were not deleted by
assumption. H10-C4 detail remains in this supplementary evidence set instead
of expanding the main chapter with case-level tables.
