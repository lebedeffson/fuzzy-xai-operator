# Terminology Audit

- `RouteAuditor`: structural route evidence, contracts, obligations, and cut.
- `RepairPlanner`: plan construction from a registered cut.
- `RepairExecutor`: execution, postconditions, and rollback.
- `RouteRecertifier`: full route reconstruction and validation.
- `ExternalPolicy`: downstream domain decision; not imported by RouteAuditor.
- `FuzzyXAI`: framework name, not a synonym for ExternalPolicy.
