# Q1 comprehension study

Status: `planned_not_run`.

This directory contains the protocol and empty participant templates. A result may be marked complete only after at least 12 independent participant records are present, consent and exclusion handling are documented, and the blinded A/B scoring script has run.

Conditions:

- A: prediction, confidence and the paired base local explanation;
- B: decision, reasons, limitations, action and expandable FuzzyXAI provenance.

Primary outcomes are correct understanding of the decision, reasons, limitations and action. Secondary outcomes are time, subjective clarity, cognitive load and overtrust. Method names are hidden and presentation order is randomized.
