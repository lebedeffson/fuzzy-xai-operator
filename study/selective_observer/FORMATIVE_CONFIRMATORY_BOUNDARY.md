# Selective observer research boundary

The public Q1 result at commit `e34e52fb8ae62ee1be043d6d5b26a0c9214a0572` is immutable. Its null and negative
results remain part of the research record.

## Formative phase

The controller, domain dictionary, explanation cards and validation-derived features may change. Only train,
validation, out-of-fold predictions, formative participants and pilot experts may be used. Formative results cannot
support confirmatory claims.

## Freeze

Before confirmation, freeze the implementation commit, controller development hash, UI hash, dictionary hash,
dataset hashes, outcomes, baselines, minimum effects, exclusions, statistical tests, stopping rule and power analysis.
The protocol manifest must be signed or independently timestamped before any confirmatory test result is opened.

## Confirmation

Use new datasets or untouched test partitions, new participants and new experts. Test outcomes may be opened once.
No method change is allowed after opening. A null result remains null and removes the corresponding positive claim.

No script in this package creates participant, reviewer or expert responses.
