# CH6 medical protocol lock

- FuzzyXAI P19 core and scientific operators are frozen.
- All final scientific quantities come from public `explain_one()` results.
- Raw data, credentials, full checkpoints and absolute local paths are excluded.
- Model selection and calibration use train/validation only; final tests remain
  sealed until their domain configuration is frozen.
- ECG is an independent PTB-XL portability experiment, not an Averkin lineage.
- Brain work is an open-data continuation of the neuroanatomical recognition
  task, not subject-level or clinical validation.
- Cross-domain conclusions concern contract applicability to evaluated tasks,
  never universal medical validation or diagnostic improvement.
